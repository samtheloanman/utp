02 — Concept

Built on one idea

Nothing in the mark is decorative. Every form earns its place.

The ring — one world

A continuous circle with no beginning or end: the shared planet and the common rules we all live under. It holds every issue, at every scale.

Two spheres — two voices

People on either side of a question. Offset and balanced rather than opposed — dialogue, not a tug of war. Distinct tones keep both sides equally present.

Balance — the outcome

The spheres settle on a diagonal axis through the ring's center: weight given to both sides until the people decide. The mark literally depicts a vote in balance.
