₿

BTC L2 Stablecoin (UBTC)

UBTC operates as a stablecoin collateralized by Bitcoin, deployed on a Bitcoin Layer 2 network (like RSK or Stacks). It provides financial sovereignty, breaking away from compromised L1s, allowing transparent minting, burning, and collateral management.

🤖

AI Debate Fact-Engine

An off-chain intelligence layer powered by LLMs. It continuously ingests news and legislative agendas, processing them to generate highly objective "Pro" vs "Con" debate arguments, eliminating human bias and media spin.

🗳️

Legislature UI & Polling

An interactive Web3 dashboard where users read AI-generated debates and cast cryptographically secure votes on global issues. Combines on-chain and off-chain data to map verified public sentiment accurately.
